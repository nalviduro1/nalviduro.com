How an engagement runs

Three gates. At each one you get a document you can take anywhere, and a decision.

  1. Intake
    A 30-minute call and a one-page problem statement. Decides whether we work together.
  2. G1 · Discovery
    What you sell, to whom, and what the site or pipeline must do. Includes the buy-vs-build map: what to rent (a booking tool, a ticketing service) and what to own. Decides scope.
  3. G2 · Plan
    Backlog, dates, cost. Decides go or no-go. Build can be yours, mine, or a contractor's.
  4. G3 · Launch
    Live product, a runbook, thirty days of fixes. Then handover: you own it, or I keep running it on pakëzdritë, where accounts, payments, email and ticketing already exist.

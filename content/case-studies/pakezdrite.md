**Problem.** Tampa Bay's Albanian community had no single place for jobs, businesses and events in both languages.

**What was built.** Bilingual listings and postings, user accounts with moderation, Stripe payments, automated notifications, SEO plan with Search Console measurement, daily business shout-outs on social. Hosted on Railway; LLC formed 2026-10-17.

**Numbers.** Live since 2026-09-25. TODO: listings, postings, sign-ups, moderation turnaround.

**What's reusable for clients.** Accounts, moderation, payments, email, SEO plumbing — the pieces every client site needs and no client should pay to rebuild. Client sites run as tenants of this platform.
